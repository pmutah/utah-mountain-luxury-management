# Feature registry

Status words: `sample` (built on stand-in data), `live` (uses real accounts), `planned`.

Existing expenses, bookings, receipts, and construction records stay in Cloudflare KV. New market data will go in D1 (`web/migrations/0001_market.sql`) and is not wired to production yet.

| Area | Feature | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data safety | KV backup and integrity baseline | live | Private folder outside the repo. Re-run `uml-data-backups/backup-kv.mjs`. |
| Market | Top 10 comps per house | sample | Pin and hide are saved in this browser. A hidden comp is replaced from the bench. Live comps replace the samples when a key exists. |
| Market | 90-day rate band and percentile | sample | Gold line is our ask. Band is open comps. | 
| Market | Next 30, 60, and 90 nights | sample | Our booked share uses the live calendar. The comp share is still the sample set. | 
| Market | What-if nightly ask | sample | A typed ask is judged against the floor, the ceiling, and the 8% cap. When that night asks for more than one night on our booking page, the line says so. An open night also shows the direct charge that page would use, before tax. It does not change the published rate. |
| Market | Listing opportunities | sample | Titles are on our booking page. A lone weekend night waits until the last week. Photos and card payment stay open. Nothing posts to Airbnb or VRBO. |
| Market | Shadow rate moves, 8% cap, holiday lock, floors | sample | Does not write Airbnb, VRBO, or Hospitable. |
| Market | Daily shadow run | sample | One text covers River, Ranch, and Lindon. Booked, closed, and paused nights stay at the published rate. Promotions follow the fill rules. Each finished Denver day is saved on this browser, up to seven. The text is shown and not sent. |
| Market | Hospitable calendar push | planned | Seven saved shadow days and a write token are both required. The write stays closed. |
| Booking | Our own direct site, beside Hospitable Direct | sample | `/book` quotes the host net night by night, shows the published house (sleeps, arrival, parking, nearby, the house notes for the hot tub, the septic, and the river, and to call 911 first), and applies the 20% fill rate when the year is short. The photo caption says when the picture is a rendering. The address opens a map. The nearby list is the published guest guide. A published website opens, and the other places open a map. Open arrivals lists the next three shortest stays the page will quote. The market desk shows those same arrivals, the lengths the first one can quote, and the add-ons that arrival can request, with no prices. Another house that will quote the same dates is listed, and choosing it does not send a request. A check-in that is too short offers the shortest stay that night can start. A check-in that can run longer offers the lengths that quote, up to 28 nights, and choosing one does not send a request. Picking an open arrival fills the dates and does not send a request. Closed nights and booked nights are marked on the calendar. A party larger than the house is refused, and an arrival that needs more than one night says so. Two or more guests see how the stay total splits, and those shares add up to the stay. The page address reopens that house, those dates, that party, and the add-ons that are checked. When the direct stay is below the Airbnb guest total, the page says so. The request stays off until the guest count fits and the rules are read. The page also lists the checkout steps and says the door code opens on the guest phone the day before check-in, after the house rules are read. An earlier arrival shows only when the night before is open, and it stays a request. The same page can add that request, a later checkout, the Ranch hot tub, or a River grocery drop. Checking one does not charge a card or send the request. The page can print a house guide. The paper leaves off the request form, a door code, and a card charge. |
| Guest | Trip portal, lock codes, upsells | sample | The stay app shows the trip steps, an add-on menu, and a checkout list the guest can check off. A trip sheet names check-in, checkout, the address, the nearby places, the house notes, the checkout steps, and to call 911 first. A published website is named on the paper. A door-code or Wi-Fi note, including a checkout step or a nearby place, is left off. The paper copy leaves the door code and Wi-Fi on the phone. The River grocery drop can include a list that stays on that phone. Nothing is ordered or charged. The door code stays hidden until the guest reads the house rules on that phone. Those taps stay on the phone and do not sign the stay agreement or change the reservation. Early arrival and late checkout appear only when that night is open. Each stay on this list says which add-ons that stay can request, with no price, and this page does not send the request. The app stays off until it is turned on for that house. |
| Ops | Occupancy file | live | Overview can download taken nights for Ranch, Lindon, and River. Each block says the house is unavailable. Names, payouts, and cancelled stays stay out. Downloading does not change a booking. |
| Ops | Cleaner desk for the next two weeks | sample | `#/clean` lists turnovers in English or Spanish, with checkout at 11:00 AM. The work order names the address, where to park, the published house notes, such as the hot tub and the septic, and the checkout steps the guest was asked to do. Those steps are not the cleaner checklist. The door code stays off the paper. The photo line stays open until a room photo is attached in this tab. A fix note stays in this browser and shows on the agents brief. Print makes a paper copy of the jobs. Checks, notes, and the paper copy do not create a task or change a booking or an expense. |
| Owner | Todd portal and statements | sample | `#/owner` is a read-only Ranch and River statement after the 20% fee. Year to date lists each month, with Ranch and River on that line. The two shares add up to the month, the months add up to Todd’s share, and each house’s lines add up to that house. Print makes a paper copy. Download saves that same statement as a spreadsheet. A separate count shows stays still in the house and stays still to come, with nights left, where those stays were booked, and the next open stretch on each house. A stay that is not Airbnb or VRBO keeps its source name. A blocked night stays off that stretch. Names and payouts stay off that count, and it is not in the spreadsheet. The next open arrival on Ranch and River lists the lengths it can quote and the add-ons it can request, with no prices. Lindon, household, and construction stay off that page. |
| AI | Agents hub | sample | `#/agents` reads a nightly brief from the calendar and the shadow run: raises, promotions, and booked nights left alone, with no dollar amounts. Checkout lines name 11:00 AM, and a same-day turn also names the 4:00 PM arrival. A cleaner fix note from this browser is included. The brief also says what the guest was asked to do before checkout. The brief also says where the Ranch and River stays still on the books were booked, keeping the source name when it is not Airbnb or VRBO, the next open stretch on each house, the next open arrival, the lengths that arrival can quote, and the add-ons that arrival can request, with no dollar amounts and no names. A blocked night stays off the stretch and the arrival. A printed trip sheet keeps the house notes. The Hospitable write stays closed. It opens Co-host and Build and lists the shared jobs. It does not claim a job, send a text, or change the books. |
